Project Overview

A third-semester project at FH JOANNEUM: design and build a complete Android app of our own, from the data model to the UI.

The Assignment

Project members: Manuel Leiter, Stefan König & Nikola Vasic

Over the course of the third semester we were given the task of developing an app of our own. We built ours in Android Studio using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Since we study business informatics, a budget tracking app was the obvious fit: users record income and expenses clearly and stay in control of their finances at all times.

At a Glance

  • Context University project, 3rd semester, FH JOANNEUM
  • Team 3 students
  • Stack Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Android Studio, Microsoft SQL
  • Collaboration GitLab — branches, merges and reviews
  • Focus Mobile development, data modelling, personal finance

Features

Open a feature to see the matching screen on the phone.

Secure sign-up and sign-in; credentials are stored encrypted.

A dashboard with income, expenses and balance. Transactions are deleted with a swipe.

Quickly add income or expenses, with categories to keep things tidy.

Filter by description, amount, category or date.

Every transaction of the month at a glance — the basis for a closer look at where the money went.

Converts gross to net including tax and social insurance.

“What if” analyses for investments made through dollar-cost averaging.

Set savings goals and follow the progress, including how much is still to go.

Level progress based on how much the user saves, plus the logout.

Challenges

The four things that made this project genuinely hard.

Kotlin & Jetpack Compose

A new technology — getting to grips with modern Android patterns and best practices.

Wiring up the database

Every screen needed different data — designing the data flows and repositories behind them.

GitLab

Our first real team workflow: branches, merges and reviews, and keeping collaboration clean.

Time investment

Running alongside other projects and exams — planning, prioritising and staying focused.

What I Took Away

App & database development

A complete Android app in Kotlin and Compose, connected to Microsoft SQL — from the data model all the way to the UI.

Teamwork

Communication, splitting up the work and versioning with GitLab to collaborate efficiently.

Time management & organisation

Planning effectively across several parallel projects and exam periods.
